About Pit Stop Automotive — Austin's Mobile Mechanic

Pit Stop Automotive is a mobile-first auto service company proudly born and based in Austin, Texas. We started with a simple mission: to make vehicle care simple, fast, and transparent for busy Austin residents.

We understand that your time is valuable. That's why we bring our professional mechanic and detailing services directly to your home, office, or wherever you are in the Austin metro area. No more scheduling around shop hours, no more sitting in waiting rooms, and no more surprise charges.

Our Mission

To provide convenient, honest, and affordable mobile auto care to the Austin community. Whether you need a quick oil change, brake service, engine diagnostics, or a full car detail — we've got your back, wherever you are.
